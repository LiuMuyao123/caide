A serving technique is not a constant. CAIDE models each as a transformation of the physical state and re-measures its effect at every batch. The line is the cost multiplier vs an unoptimised baseline — below 1.0× it helps, above it hurts. Toggle techniques to compare.

A closed-form demand-response projection. With unit cost falling and demand responding with price elasticity ε, total spend moves as (c/c₀)1−ε. Above ε = 1 the Jevons regime appears: cheaper tokens raise total spend, because volume grows faster than price falls.
